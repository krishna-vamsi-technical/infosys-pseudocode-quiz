Mock Test Instructions

📝 Assessment Pattern

  • The assessment contains 30 pseudocode questions systematically selected across all core Infosys SE topics.
  • Each question has 4 options with only one correct answer.
  • Topics evaluated: Arithmetic Operators, Relational & Logical, Bitwise, Conditionals, Loops, Functions, Recursion, and Hybrid problems.

⏱️ Time Limits

  • You have 90 seconds (1.5 minutes) per question.
  • Total test time: 45 minutes maximum.
  • If the per-question timer expires, the question is auto-skipped as unanswered.
  • If total time expires, the test is auto-submitted.

🧭 Navigation

  • Navigation is one-way only — you cannot go back to previous questions.
  • Click "Next Question" to advance. Click "Submit Test" on the last question.
  • You can skip a question by clicking Next without selecting an option.

📊 Scoring

  • +1 mark for each correct answer.
  • No negative marking for wrong or unanswered questions.
  • Results with full answer review and topic analytics are shown immediately after submission.

🔒 Anti-Cheat Rules

  • The test runs in full-screen mode. Exiting fullscreen triggers a warning.
  • Tab switching and window blur are strictly monitored and logged.
  • Right-click, copy-paste, and developer keys are disabled during the test.
  • The test cannot be paused — complete it in one sitting.

📝 Practice Format

  • 30 pseudocode questions covering all core Infosys recruitment topics.
  • Each question has 4 options with only one correct answer.
  • No timer — take as long as you need on each question.

💡 Instant Feedback

  • After selecting an option, you'll immediately see if your answer is correct or wrong.
  • The correct answer and a detailed explanation will be shown.
  • You can change your answer before clicking "Next Question".
  • The explanation stays visible until you navigate to the next question.

📊 At the End

  • Your total score and topic-wise performance are shown.
  • Full review of all questions with explanations is available.
